  • 1. Unique Identity: Distinct Hangul alphabet, rich culture, and dynamic lifestyle.

  • 3. Smart Travel: Papago & Naver Map ensure effortless local navigation.
  • 4. Heritage & Future: Historic royal palaces coexist beside iconic skyscrapers.

📌 Destination Highlights

Seoul

The bustling heart of modern trends and royal heritage.

  • Top Spot: Gyeongbokgung Palace & N Seoul Tower
  • Must Try: Korean BBQ & Street Food at Gwangjang Market

Busan

A dynamic seaside city known for beaches and fresh seafood.

  • Top Spot: Haeundae Beach & Gamcheon Culture Village
  • Must Try: Dwaeji Gukbap (Pork Rice Soup)

Gyeongju

An open-air museum filled with ancient royal Silla heritage.

  • Top Spot: Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond, Bulguksa Temple
  • Must Try: Hwangnam-ppang (Red Bean Pastry)

🗓️ Suggested 7-Day Classic Route

Day 1 - 3: Seoul

Palaces, shopping districts, futuristic landmarks, and neon nightscapes.

Day 4: Jeonju or Gyeongju

Immerse in traditional Hanok architecture or ancient historical sites.

Day 5 - 6: Busan

Relax by the ocean, visit coastal temples, and enjoy vibrant fish markets.

Day 7: Jeju Island

Unwind with scenic volcanic landscapes and coastal drives.

💡 Essential Travel Tips Before You Go

T-Money Card

Get a universal transit card at any convenience store for subways, buses, and taxis.

Navigation Apps

Download Naver Map or KakaoMap; Google Maps has limited walking directions in Korea.

Zero Tipping

Tipping is not practiced or expected in restaurants, taxis, or hotels.

24/7 Safety

Cities remain safe late into the night with bustling night markets and brightly lit streets.

Public Wi-Fi

Free, high-speed Wi-Fi is accessible across public transit, cafes, and main attractions.
