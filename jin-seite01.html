Top Recommended Cities to Visit in Korea

Korea offers an unforgettable blend of centuries-old tradition and forward-thinking modern culture. From the bustling nightlife of Seoul to the serene coastal landscapes of Jeju Island, each region brings its own unique charm and hidden stories waiting to be explored.

1. Seoul

Experience the vibrant pulse of South Korea's bustling capital city. Modern skyscrapers stand in breathtaking harmony alongside timeless historic palaces. Indulge in neon-lit night markets, world-class shopping, and endless street food options.

2. Busan

Discover the ultimate coastal getaway with stunning ocean views and golden beaches. Savor fresh local seafood at iconic fish markets and stroll through colorful hillside villages. Feel the energetic rhythm of Korea's most famous harbor city.

3. Gyeongju

Step into a living museum where ancient Silla Dynasty heritage lives on. Walk among tranquil royal tombs, historic temples, and serene lotus ponds under starry skies. A timeless journey for lovers of history, culture, and nature.

4. Jeonju

Immerse yourself in authentic Korean tradition and world-renowned gastronomy. Wander through hundreds of classic Hanok houses while wearing traditional clothing. Taste legendary Bibimbap and delicious local snacks at every corner.

5. Jeju

Discover Jeju’s iconic treasures under sunrise light at Seongsan Ilchulbong. Meet symbolic Dol Hareubang stone statues guarding the path, and witness the incredible Haenyeo female divers preserving island tradition. A captivating blend of natural beauty and rich cultural heritage.

💡 Essential Travel Tips Before You Go

  1. T-Money Card: Get a universal transit card at any convenience store for subways, buses, and taxis.
  2. Navigation Apps: Download Naver Map or KakaoMap; Google Maps has limited walking directions in Korea.
  3. Zero Tipping: Tipping is not practiced or expected in restaurants, taxis, or hotels.
  4. 24/7 Safety: Cities remain safe late into the night with bustling night markets and brightly lit streets.
  5. Public Wi-Fi: Free, high-speed Wi-Fi is accessible across public transit, cafes, and main attractions.

🗓️ Suggested 7-Day Classic Route

We recommend taking at least 1 to 2 weeks of vacation to fully experience Korea. Compared to the high standard of living, accommodation, food, and public transit are remarkably affordable. If you are staying for a week or two, here is a recommended itinerary:

Day 1 - 3: Seoul

Palaces, shopping districts, futuristic landmarks, and neon nightscapes.

Day 4: Jeonju or Gyeongju

Immerse in traditional Hanok architecture or ancient historical sites.

Day 5 - 6: Busan

Relax by the ocean, visit coastal temples, and enjoy vibrant fish markets.

Day 7: Jeju Island

Unwind with scenic volcanic landscapes and coastal drives.
